# Property dossiers and evidence

Use **Property** for the shared object inventory. A person's declaration observations remain in their profile; the inventory does not add both collections together.

## One card per recorded object

Choose **Add property**. Only the title is required. Complete the address, country, cadastral number, registry number and object type when known. The reference person provides context; dated **Rights** identify recorded owners and users.

The optional groups contain the initial owner, acquisition date and grounds, a source, and the legal holder category. For company property, enter the company name and registration number. A person's interest in a company does not establish personal ownership of the company's assets.

Open the dossier to add:

- **Rights:** ownership, use, lease, possession or management; holder, share, period, grounds and sources.
- **Transfers:** gift, sale, succession, registration, division or another transition; parties, effective/contract/registration dates, share, consideration and documents.
- **Encumbrances:** seizure, mortgage, prohibition, pledge or easement; beneficiary, period, authority, reference and sources.
- **Cash-flow records:** payer and recipient, accounts, amount, currency, date, transaction reference and supporting records.
- **Claims:** proposed remedy, claimant, grounds, amount, evidence still needed, objections, risks and time limits requiring professional review.

**Add source** on an individual record opens a document editor with that object and fact already selected. A file is optional: a requested or missing source can be tracked before a copy is obtained. Several documents can support the same fact.

## Historical records and divided plots

Link a new card to its **Previous object card**, or give related cards a **Shared economic asset key**. Several child plots can refer to one predecessor. These links group observations; they do not establish legal identity.

Review the identifiers and documents. To include an economic group in the inventory valuation:

1. Choose one **Main card: group valuation**, with the value of the whole reviewed group.
2. Mark every other card **Reference only**.
3. Keep the current valuation on a current card, rather than a historical entry.

Unresolved groups and unlinked cards with matching cadastral/registration numbers are excluded from totals. The same claimant and claim type across related cards triggers an overlap warning. Check the actual subject matter before combining or asserting demands.

The valuation summary covers the full inventory independently of filters. It separates currencies, individuals, companies and unclassified ownership. It describes whole objects or reviewed groups, not an inheritance share or a person's net worth.

## Estate inventory

Expand **Estate inventory**, then select **Person for estate inventory** and **Category at death**. The categories distinguish recorded ownership at death, recorded lifetime disposals, company property and insufficient information. **Use date of death** sets the history snapshot to an exact recorded death date.

An approximate death date or uncertain right is not converted into a definite estate classification. Recorded ownership, a transfer and a possible claim remain separate observations; the app does not determine succession rights or transaction validity.

## Asset map and cash flows

The **Asset and transfer map** groups related cards and shows their recorded transition chains with links to source copies. Open any object to inspect its full ledger.

The **Cash-flow map** shows payments separately from valuations and proposed claims. Its totals include dated, corroborated observations with an available corroborated source. Future, unsupported and potentially repeated payments are excluded. Repeated transaction references are checked across the whole inventory, including records outside the current filter. Give genuinely different transactions different references after reviewing the records. Different currencies are never converted or combined.

## Evidence register and originals

In **Documents**, select **Evidence register**. Record the holder/custodian, original/copy form, fact supported, route for obtaining the evidence, original location and handover inventory reference. Receipt, return and certification dates are optional. Attach the inventory itself as a **Handover inventory** source.

Use the same library for death and kinship documents, probate files and inventories, wills, powers of attorney, registry extracts, contracts, medical/treatment records, witness lists, correspondence, company extracts and bank statements. Witness profiles can also hold attributed witness records.

Keep the provenance of digital copies in the source's reference and retrieval fields. Store originals once; reuse attachments and image annotations for multiple people, properties or facts. Available image copies can be cropped while retaining the original. A source's stated supported fact is separate from its verification status.

**Export register CSV** exports the current filtered selection. ZIP backups preserve dossiers, links and attached copies; JSON exports describe the records without file contents. Complete biographies include the person's linked property history and entered particulars.
